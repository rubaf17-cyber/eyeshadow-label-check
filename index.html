Talc OH band compared with NIST talc

This sample (ATR) NIST talc, CAS 14807-96-6 (diffuse reflectance, log 1/R)

Only the OH stretching region is compared: the NIST spectrum was measured by diffuse reflectance of neat powder, so its fingerprint region (below 1300 cm⁻¹) is distorted and not comparable with ATR. A lower correlation can indicate other OH-bearing minerals, for example mica (Al–OH near 3626 cm⁻¹).

Read before using the results

  • Detection thresholds were set on 14 pans measured on one diamond-ATR instrument (single spectrum per pan). Validate on your own instrument before relying on them.
  • “Not detected” means below the detection rule, not proven absent. Kaolin and mica rules are provisional; synthetic mica has no OH band and cannot be seen in the OH region.
  • The NIST talc spectrum was measured by diffuse reflectance of neat powder, so only its OH stretching region is compared.
  • Stearate type is read from the carboxylate band position only; it indicates the metal, it does not quantify it.
